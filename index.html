01services

Analysis services for genetics research.

Each project ends with files you can reuse and a report you can cite: alignments, tree files, figures and the reasoning behind them.

Builds phylogenetic trees

Alignment, model choice and tree building in MEGA or BEAST, delivered with FASTA and tree files ready for FigTree.

Maps haplotypes and diversity

Haplotype identification, molecular statistics and phylogenetic networks with DnaSP, DAMBE and Network.

Predicts epitopes and HLA binding

BLASTp screening against the human proteome, BepiPred epitopes, NetMHCpan binding and IEDB population coverage.

Checks results against the literature

Every finding compared with recent peer-reviewed studies from PubMed and reference sequences from NCBI.

Writes and formats research documents

Scientific reports, literature reviews, proofreading, APA formatting and referencing for theses and articles.

Teaches biology and genetics

Natural sciences teaching at secondary school, and clear explanations of genetics and cell biology for students.

  1. 01

    Collect the sequences

    I pull sequences from GenBank or take yours as FASTA, then check names, lengths and quality before any analysis starts.

    NCBI GenBank · FASTA

  2. 02

    Align every position

    MEGA lines the sequences up so each column compares the same site. Gaps and variable sites become visible.

    MEGA · DAMBE

  3. 03

    Build trees and networks

    Phylogenetic trees in MEGA or BEAST, haplotypes and diversity statistics in DnaSP, median-joining networks in Network.

    MEGA · BEAST · DnaSP · Network · FigTree

  4. 04

    Write the report

    Methods, figures, files and an interpretation compared with recent PubMed studies, formatted to APA and ready to cite.

    PubMed · APA style

03research

Research on SARS-CoV-2 and HPV16.

Published 2022

SARS-CoV-2 genes, phylogeny and haplotypes

Master II thesis · LBEIG laboratory, USTHB, Algiers · 2020 to 2021

An inventory of the genes identified in the coronavirus behind acute respiratory distress syndrome, analysed with bioinformatics tools. I ran every analysis: phylogenetic trees, haplotypes, molecular statistics and phylogenetic networks.

  • Graded 18.5/20, among the best final-year projects of 2020/2021
  • Published in Genetics and Biodiversity Journal (2022), with me as co-author
Open the journal

In preparation

HPV16 E6 immunoinformatics

Independent research · being prepared for publication

A screen of the HPV16 E6 oncoprotein for molecular mimicry and immunogenicity: BLASTp against the human proteome, B-cell epitopes with BepiPred, MHC-I and MHC-II affinity with NetMHCpan, and IEDB coverage of HLA types in North African and European populations.
