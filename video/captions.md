# Posting copy for the portfolio video

Written with the create-viral-content skill's rules: the hook lands in the first line, every claim
is specific and true, there are no hype words, and the post ends on an instruction, not a request
for likes. The posts are in Yasmine's voice (first person), because it's her portfolio. A version
for posting as the site's developer is at the end.

Files:
- `yasmine-portfolio-16x9.mp4`: 1920x1080, 48 s. For LinkedIn, YouTube, X and the GitHub README.
- `yasmine-portfolio-9x16.mp4`: 1080x1920, 48 s. For Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts and stories.
- `cover-16x9.jpg` and `cover-9x16.jpg`: thumbnails or covers.

Both videos have an original soundtrack (synthesized for this video, so nothing to license or
credit): a 101 BPM electronic track in A minor timed to the edit, plus sound effects synced to the
screen. The captions are burned in, so the videos also work with sound off. The soundtrack alone
is in `soundtrack.m4a`. If you post to Instagram or TikTok and add trending audio there, lower the
original sound in the app's mixer rather than muting it, so the clicks and bursts still land.

## LinkedIn (16:9 video)

My portfolio is built from the SARS-CoV-2 spike gene.

The double helix on my new site carries the first 42 bases of the spike gene I studied for my
Master's thesis in genetics. Move your cursor over it and the base pairs come apart as lines of
the code I use every day: Biopython, BLAST, MEGA, NetMHCpan.

Further down there's a small sequence lab, and a second section for the other half of my work:
AI-made short videos, social media management and ad campaigns for brands. Paste any DNA sequence and it returns the GC content,
reverse complement, protein and longest open reading frame, then writes the same steps in Python.

That's the work I take on for researchers and students: phylogenetic trees, haplotype networks,
epitope prediction and research writing, each delivered with its data files and a report you can cite.

Sequences waiting for analysis? Send them to bzdyasmine97@gmail.com.
